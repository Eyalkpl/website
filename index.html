About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in mathematics at UC Berkeley, working with Gabe Goldberg. My research interests lie in set theory—particularly forcing, large cardinals, and inner model theory. I completed my PhD in Tel-Aviv university under the supervision of Moti Gitik (thesis).

You can reach me at: eyalkaplan 'at' berkeley.edu.
