About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in mathematics at UC Berkeley, working with Gabe Goldberg . My research interests lie in set theory—particularly forcing, large cardinals, and inner model theory. I completed my PhD under the supervision of Moti Gitik .

You can reach me at: eyalkaplan 'at' berkeley.edu
